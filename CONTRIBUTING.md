# Contributing

This is an independently authored product case study. Suggestions and critical feedback are welcome.

## Standards
- Keep claims source-backed or label them as hypotheses / proposals.
- Never add fabricated customer interviews, quotes, adoption, revenue, or outcome metrics.
- Do not publish confidential operational or personal data.
- Cite product and policy claims with dated primary sources where possible.
- Record material product decisions and their revisit triggers.
- Keep synthetic prototype data clearly labelled.

## Changes
For substantive changes, explain the evidence or reasoning and update the relevant decision / source record.
