## Evidence rules

A deliverable being written does not make its underlying product hypothesis validated. Label every claim as fact, observation, validated finding, hypothesis, proposal, or unknown. Record the source, date, participant context (where applicable), confidence, and implications.

## Definition of a meaningful next release

The next meaningful release is not more documentation volume. It is a research update that contains:
1. A documented recruitment log and completed interview records, with appropriate consent.
2. A synthesis of recurring workflows, pain points, workarounds, and consequences.
3. Counter-evidence and rejected assumptions.
4. A revised target segment and problem statement, or an explicit decision to pivot.
5. A traceable explanation of how findings changed product scope.
