## Status definitions
- **Unvalidated:** assumption exists; evidence not yet collected.
- **Supported:** evidence points in favour but important uncertainty remains.
- **Validated for this context:** sufficient evidence for a defined segment / use case, with limitations recorded.
- **Rejected:** evidence contradicts the assumption.
- **Deferred:** not required to decide the current stage.
