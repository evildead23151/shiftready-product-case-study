## Workflow map

Document the process as it actually occurs:

1. Trigger: what starts shift preparation?
2. Inputs: people, systems, records, and physical checks involved.
3. Decision: who decides whether a vehicle is ready?
4. Failure points: where does uncertainty or delay appear?
5. Recovery: who acts, and how?
6. Outcome: what is delayed, reassigned, or lost?

## Insight format

Use the following structure for every insight:

**Insight:** concise statement.

**Evidence:** participant IDs, dated notes, observation, or artifact.

**Pattern:** how many relevant cases and in what context.

**Counter-evidence:** cases where the pattern did not occur.

**Interpretation:** what the evidence may mean.

**Confidence:** low / medium / high, with rationale.

**Product implication:** what we should consider changing.

**Open question:** what remains unknown.

## Synthesis guardrails

- Do not count multiple quotes from one person as multiple independent participants.
- Do not use a single vivid anecdote to claim market-wide frequency.
- Do not confuse a workaround with a validated product requirement.
- Keep contradictory cases.
- Segment findings by fleet operating model.
- Do not publish identifiable or confidential details without consent.
