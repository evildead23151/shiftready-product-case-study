# 17 — Final Case Study

> This is the presentation scaffold. Populate the findings only after research and prototyping have actually occurred.

## 5–7 minute narrative

### 1. Context (30–45 seconds)
Describe the chosen fleet segment and operating context with sourced facts.

### 2. Problem (45–60 seconds)
Show the actual workflow and a recent incident, using anonymised evidence and permissioned quotes.

### 3. Discovery (60–90 seconds)
Explain who was interviewed, how participants were recruited, what patterns emerged, and what contradicted the original hypothesis.

### 4. Product decision (60 seconds)
Show which opportunity was prioritised, alternatives considered, and why the product direction changed or held.

### 5. Solution (60–90 seconds)
Walk through the core workflow, requirements, edge states, and why the MVP excludes tempting features.

### 6. Measurement and execution (45–60 seconds)
Explain the outcome metric, guardrails, baseline, pilot dependencies, and roadmap gates.

### 7. Reflection (30–45 seconds)
State what remains unknown, what would change the decision, and what you learned.

## Interview questions to prepare for

- Why this market and segment?
- What evidence says the problem is real?
- Why isn't an incumbent sufficient?
- Who pays and who uses it?
- Why is this the smallest viable product?
- How would you handle stale or conflicting data?
- What is your north-star metric and how can it be gamed?
- What would make you stop building?
- What would you do with six more weeks?
- Which decision are you least confident about?

## Portfolio publication checklist

- [ ] All market and competitor claims have dated sources.
- [ ] Research sample and method are reported accurately.
- [ ] Findings distinguish quotes, observations, and interpretation.
- [ ] Counter-evidence is included.
- [ ] Screenshots show actual prototype state and synthetic-data labels.
- [ ] Metrics use explicit definitions and no invented outcomes.
- [ ] The final story explains trade-offs and changed decisions.
- [ ] Sensitive participant and company information is removed or permissioned.
