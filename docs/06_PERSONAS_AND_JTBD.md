# 06 — Personas and Jobs to Be Done

> These are **provisional role hypotheses**, not research-validated personas. Replace them with evidence after interviews.

## Persona hypothesis A — Head of Fleet Operations

**Role in buying:** potential economic buyer / sponsor.

**Job hypothesis:** keep the fleet productive while meeting delivery and operating commitments.

**Potential measures:** dispatch reliability, vehicle utilisation, cost per delivery, downtime, energy expense.

**Questions to validate:**
- Which operational metric do they own?
- How much can they spend to improve it?
- What evidence is needed for procurement?
- Which existing vendor contracts constrain adoption?

## Persona hypothesis B — Depot Manager / Dispatcher

**Role in workflow:** potential primary daily user.

**Job hypothesis:** prepare the right vehicles for each shift and resolve blockers before the cutoff.

**Potential measures:** time to establish readiness, unresolved exceptions at cutoff, manual coordination effort.

**Questions to validate:**
- What is the actual pre-shift checklist?
- Which decisions require judgement?
- What information arrives late?
- What would make a dashboard inaccurate or untrusted?

## Persona hypothesis C — Driver / Rider

**Role in workflow:** potential frontline contributor.

**Job hypothesis:** receive an operationally ready vehicle and communicate relevant issues without avoidable delay.

**Potential measures:** time waiting for assignment, repeated reporting, issue resolution.

**Questions to validate:**
- What status does the driver know versus the depot?
- How is an issue reported today?
- Does an additional app create burden?
- Is the driver the right data-entry point?

## Evidence needed before finalising personas

- actual responsibilities and decision rights;
- workflow artifacts or detailed incident walkthroughs;
- differences across fleet types and replenishment models;
- language participants use for readiness and exceptions;
- buying roles and budget authority;
- counterexamples and segment boundaries.
